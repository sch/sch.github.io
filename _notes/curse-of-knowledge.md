The [curse of knowledge] is the idea that experts rarely have the skill to be
great teachers because they visualize a beginner's point of view.

[Seeing the Invisible] is a paper that attempts to quantify just how much
knowledge is tacit in this way: it suggests that experts are both able to see
more information than beginners and they are able to make inferences that
beginners cannot.

[How to help someone use a computer] is something of a checklist in empathizing
with a beginner and can be applied to pretty much any technical skill.

Chip Morningstar, creator of Habitat at LucasArts and one-time Xanadu developer,
tells his "show-don't-tell" outlook through an [anecdote][Chip Morningstar] on
trying to explain hypertext before it was mainstream and deploying
a rich-client server architecture when it was uncommon. Part of it is a plea to
have more time to experiment and prototype before needing to create a big plan or
evangelize for funding
